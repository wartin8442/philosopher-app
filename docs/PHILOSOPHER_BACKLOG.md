# Philosopher Expansion Backlog

**Status:** Complete — all 23 coverage philosophers implemented  
**Scope:** Mostly Western philosophy  
**Goal:** Expand the five-philosopher MVP into a viable collection with broad
coverage of major philosophical traditions. This is a coverage-first backlog,
not an attempt to include every canonical philosopher.

Voice and personality decisions for these candidates are developed in
[PHILOSOPHER_VOICE_BIBLE.md](PHILOSOPHER_VOICE_BIBLE.md).

- Friedrich Nietzsche — genealogy, perspectivism, and the crisis of nihilism
- Søren Kierkegaard — Christian existentialism
- Jean-Paul Sartre — atheistic existentialism
- Albert Camus — absurdism

## Selection rules

- Prefer candidates who represent a major tradition currently absent from the
  app over candidates who add another voice to an already represented cluster.
- A philosopher may be interdisciplinary; inclusion depends on a substantial
  philosophical contribution, not their academic job title.
- Do not treat this document as an implementation sequence. Source planning,
  persona reliability, legal constraints, and likely user value should be
  reviewed before selecting the next batch.
- Living-person simulations and contemporary public figures are outside this
  backlog for now.
- Follow the curation and testing process in
  [ADDING_A_PHILOSOPHER.md](ADDING_A_PHILOSOPHER.md) for every addition.
