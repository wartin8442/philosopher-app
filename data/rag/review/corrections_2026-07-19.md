# Conservative Card Corrections — 2026-07-19

## Scope and standard

This pass reviewed the complete five-author draft-card corpus and changed only claims that could be shown to be false, materially misleading, falsely attributed, or unfairly categorical. Disputed interpretations were left unchanged unless a card presented one side as settled fact. Card status remains `draft`; this is a source-backed correction pass, not a declaration that every card has completed human editorial review.

## Aquinas

1. **Matter as the Principle of Individuation in Material Things** — restricted the claim to material substances and clarified Aquinas's two-stage account: matter initially individuates form; the particular substantial form then individuates the substance. Sources: *On Being and Essence*, chs. 2–3; [SEP, “Thomas Aquinas,” §4](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aquinas/).
2. **Unjust Human Laws Lack Full Moral Authority** — replaced the absolute “not law at all” takeaway with Aquinas's actual conscience rule, including the scandal/disturbance qualification and the stricter rule for commands contrary to divine law. Sources: [*Summa Theologiae* I-II, Q.96, a.4](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2096.htm); [SEP, “Aquinas's Moral, Political, and Legal Philosophy”](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aquinas-moral-political/).
3. **Moral Virtue Is More Than Technical Skill or a One-Off Good Deed** — corrected the false sharp separation between every virtue and every skill. Aquinas calls art an intellectual virtue in a qualified sense while distinguishing aptitude from right use. Sources: [ST I-II, Q.56](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2056.htm) and [Q.57](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2057.htm).
4. **Theological Virtues Are Gifts of Grace, Not Things You Can Earn** — preserved the core claim about faith, hope, and charity but removed the implication that cardinal/moral virtues are acquired only. Aquinas explicitly recognizes infused moral virtues. Source: [ST I-II, Q.63, aa.3–4](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2063.htm).

## Kierkegaard

1. **Truth Is Subjectivity** — clarified that subjective appropriation concerns the individual's relation to existential/religious truth; sincerity does not make false propositions true. Sources: *Concluding Unscientific Postscript*, Part Two, §2, ch. 2; [SEP, “Søren Kierkegaard,” §§2–3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kierkegaard/).
2. **A Qualitative Leap, Not a Deduction** and **Kierkegaard Did Not Write the Phrase “Leap of Faith”** — corrected the false quotation/attribution. Kierkegaard uses “leap” and “qualitative transition,” but no Danish equivalent of the English phrase “leap of faith.” Source: M. Jamie Ferreira, [“Faith and the Kierkegaardian Leap,” *Cambridge Companion to Kierkegaard*](https://doi.org/10.1017/CCOL0521471516.009), pp. 207–234.
3. **Teleological Suspension of the Ethical** — changed a general permission claim into the question actually staged by the pseudonym Johannes de silentio, and warned against treating Abraham as a reusable private-exception rule. Sources: *Fear and Trembling*, Problema I; [SEP, §3.3.1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kierkegaard/).
4. **Misreading: “Kierkegaard Was Basically a Secular Existentialist”** — removed “leap of faith” as if it were Kierkegaard's wording while preserving the card's supported account of his Christian authorship.

## Camus

1. **Living Without God: Camus's Starting Point** — distinguished Camus's practical standpoint of life without appeal from a proof or confident declaration that God does not exist. Source: [SEP, “Albert Camus,” §§2–3 and §6](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/).
2. **Camus's Sustained Opposition to Capital Punishment Had a 1944 Exception** — corrected the false “lifelong” claim. Camus temporarily supported execution of the worst collaborators during the Liberation purge debate, then moved toward clemency by early 1945. Sources: *Camus at Combat: Writing 1944–1947*; Vincent Grégoire, [“Camus, Mauriac et l'épuration (1944–1945)”](https://ojs.library.dal.ca/dfs/article/view/10164).
3. **Conditions Camus Dramatizes for Exceptional Political Killing** — retained the conditions supported by scholarship but identified them as John Foley's reconstruction from *The Rebel* and *The Just Assassins*, not a numbered checklist formally announced by Camus. Source: [SEP, §6](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/), summarizing Foley (2008), p. 93.
4. **Camus and Algeria: Anti-Colonial Reporting, but a Criticized Political Position** — corrected the false claim that Camus never publicly condemned colonial injustice. His June 1939 Kabylia reports exposed famine, poverty, and neglect; his later rejection of FLN independence and preference for federation remain open to serious criticism. Sources: “Misery of Kabylia” in *Alger républicain* (June 1939), reprinted in *Algerian Chronicles*; [SEP, §4](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/).

## Nietzsche

1. **Eternal Recurrence Has Practical Force, but Its Cosmological Status Is Disputed** and **Misreading: “Eternal Recurrence Is Only a Settled Physics Thesis”** — replaced a claimed scholarly consensus with the actual interpretive dispute. The published doctrine has clear practical force; a cosmological reading remains seriously defended. Sources: *The Gay Science* §341; *Thus Spoke Zarathustra*, Part III; [SEP, “Friedrich Nietzsche,” §6.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche/).
2. **Higher Types Are Not a Racial or National Category** — kept the correction to racial-national readings but removed the unsupported “not class” language and restored Nietzsche's genuine anti-egalitarian hierarchy of rank. Sources: *Beyond Good and Evil* §§56, 260, 287; [SEP, “Nietzsche's Moral and Political Philosophy,” §§2 and 4](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche-moral-political/).
3. **Misreading: “Nietzsche Was a Proto-Nazi or Anti-Semite”** — preserved Nietzsche's opposition to organized anti-Semitism and nationalism while removing the single-cause story that blamed his entire Nazi reception on his sister. Also added a warning against using this correction to sanitize troubling passages. Sources: *Beyond Good and Evil* §251; *Ecce Homo*, “Why I Am So Wise” §3; [SEP, §1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche/).
4. **Nietzsche Is Not a Nazi Exterminationist, but His Anti-Egalitarianism Is Real** — replaced an unfairly exculpatory card with a two-sided correction: no racial extermination program, but real attacks on equality and pity and real praise of hierarchy, exploitation, and culturally productive suffering. Sources: *Beyond Good and Evil* §§225, 257, 259–260; *The Antichrist* §2; [SEP, “Nietzsche's Moral and Political Philosophy”](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche-moral-political/).

## Sartre

1. **Conscious Experience Is Prereflectively Aware of Itself** — replaced “completely transparent to itself” with the more precise claim that experience is prereflectively self-aware, while reflective claims about the enduring Ego can be mistaken. Sources: *The Transcendence of the Ego*; *Being and Nothingness*, Introduction; [SEP, “Jean-Paul Sartre,” §2](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sartre/).
2. **In *Being and Nothingness*, Conflict Structures Relations with Others** — periodized the conflict thesis and added Sartre's later analysis of reciprocity in the group-in-fusion. Sources: *Being and Nothingness*, Part Three; *Critique of Dialectical Reason*, Book I, Part II; [SEP, §§4.3 and 6.3](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sartre/).

## Recommended RAG retest

1. Rebuild the retrieval index from the corrected card files; otherwise the vector store may continue serving stale text.
2. Re-run the exact baseline stress prompts with the same model, generation settings, retrieval depth, and hardware/runtime.
3. Compare at least four metrics: factual answer score, citation/source-entailment score, retrieval hit rate for the corrected cards, and end-to-end latency (median and p95).
4. Inspect failures by layer: whether the right card was retrieved, whether the model used it faithfully, and whether the citation actually supports the generated sentence.
5. Treat improvement as credible only if accuracy rises on the fixed prompts without a material regression on unchanged prompts or unacceptable p95 latency.
